Evaluation

Task metrics and one overall score.

OmniRNA reports task-level performance for fitness, structure, and evolution, together with one transparent aggregate score.

Fitness

Reported metrics are Spearman correlation and R2. The primary fitness score is the mean Spearman correlation across datasets and evaluation splits.

Structure

Reported metrics are TM-score, RMSD, and secondary-structure F1. The primary structure score is mean TM-score.

Evolution

Reported metrics are quartet accuracy and covariation macro-F1. The primary evolution score is the arithmetic mean of these two values.

Overall

The overall score is the arithmetic mean of mapped fitness score, structure score, and evolution score.
