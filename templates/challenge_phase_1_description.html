Public Development Phase

This phase uses the public development-test split and supports iteration during the competition. Scores shown on this leaderboard are public and are intended for model development and comparison.

Participants may submit prediction files for the development split according to the required format. The public leaderboard reports task metrics for RNA fitness prediction, RNA structure prediction, and RNA evolutionary reasoning, together with the overall score.

Competition end date: 1 June 2026.
