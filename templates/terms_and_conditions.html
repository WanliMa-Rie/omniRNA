Terms and Conditions

  • Participants may use public pretrained models and public unlabeled RNA sequence resources.
  • Participants must disclose all external data, pretrained models, and compute resources used.
  • Participants may not use external labeled data that overlap with the released benchmark.
  • Participants may not use any information derived from hidden test sets.
  • Participants may not use information derived from RNA tertiary structures released after the test freeze date.
  • Final leaderboard eligibility requires runnable Docker inference code, model weights, a short method sheet, and a complete external resource statement.

The benchmark uses public RNA sequences, public structure data, and public family annotations. It does not include personally identifiable information, clinical records, or patient-level data.

The challenge focuses on predictive modeling on public benchmark data. It does not ask participants to generate harmful biological sequences or optimize pathogenic function.
