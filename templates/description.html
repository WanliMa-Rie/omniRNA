Overview

OmniRNA evaluates whether models learn useful and general RNA representations.

The benchmark centers on three biological capabilities: predicting mutational fitness, recovering RNA structure, and reasoning about evolutionary constraints. Public development results support iteration; hidden final results determine the official ranking.

Fitness

Variant-effect prediction is scored with rank-aware and regression-aware metrics.

Structure

Participants recover both secondary pairing patterns and tertiary geometry from sequence.

Evolution

The benchmark tests whether models preserve functional signals across divergent RNA families.

OmniRNA is designed for reproducible evaluation on public benchmark data and uses one shared leaderboard schema across all reported metrics.
